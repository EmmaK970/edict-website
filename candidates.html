Hasnat Sujon

Doctoral Candidate – Project 13

📍 University of Galway

Hasnat is working on developing a guideline for culturally appropriate dissemination of clinical trials (EDICT Project # 13), under the supervision of Dr. Sinéad Hynes and Dr. Eimear Morrissey at the University of Galway, Ireland.

Hasnat is a medical doctor and a clinical trialist with ~10 years' experience leading the set-up, execution, and close-out of 10+ clinical trials (Phase II–III; vaccines, infectious disease) across multi-country settings, working with industry sponsors (Moderna, GSK) and academic investigators (NIH, WHO, Johns Hopkins). He completed an MSc in Clinical Trials from the London School of Hygiene and Tropical Medicine (Commonwealth Scholar) and an MPH (Epidemiology) from University College Dublin. He has published in leading peer-reviewed journals and has secured competitive research funding from NIH, Sida, and WHO.

Maeve Herlihy

Doctoral Candidate – Project 10

📍 UMC Utrecht

Maeve holds a BSc in Public Health Sciences from University College Cork and an MSc in Epidemiology from the London School of Hygiene and Tropical Medicine. Her research experience spans both quantitative and qualitative methods. She previously worked at the HRB Clinical Research Facility at University College Cork, where her work focused on clinical trial design tools, reporting guidelines and pragmatic trial design principles. She was also part of the PRECIS-3 research team, a trial design tool aimed at helping trialists match design decisions to the intended purpose of a trial.

Maeve will be working on Project 10, Methodological and Operational Tools for Enhancing EDI in Clinical Trials, as part of Work Package 3. Her research will focus on evaluating approaches to enhance equity, diversity and inclusion (EDI) in clinical trials and developing practical guidance to support their use.
